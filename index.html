Dumb Charades

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Dumb Charades ("the App"), published by Mischief Labs, handles information when you use the App.

Dumb Charades is a local, offline party game designed to be played on a single device. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core functionality.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

2. Information We Collect

Dumb Charades does not collect or transmit personal information to Mischief Labs or to third-party servers through the App.

The App does not require:

  • Account registration
  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera access
  • Payment information

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores certain game-related preferences and gameplay information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Game settings and preferences
  • Sound and haptic preferences
  • Theme preferences
  • Gameplay configuration
  • Previously used prompt identifiers used to reduce repetition

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Removing the App or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

4. Motion and Device Sensors

Dumb Charades can use the device's motion sensors to support its optional tilt-based gameplay controls.

Sensor readings are processed locally on the device to detect the configured tilt gesture. These readings are not recorded, stored, uploaded, or shared with Mischief Labs or third parties.

5. Audio and Haptics

The App may play short sound effects and provide haptic feedback during gameplay when these features are enabled in the App's settings.

The App does not record conversations or use the device microphone for gameplay.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of Dumb Charades does not use advertising networks, analytics services, user accounts, cloud databases, payment services, or other third-party services that collect personal information through the App.

7. Children's Privacy

Dumb Charades does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the App does not require an account or the submission of personal information, its core gameplay can be used without providing personal data.

8. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit personal information to our servers, there is no personal information collected by us through the App that requires server-side storage or transmission.

Information stored locally by the App remains subject to the security of the device and operating system on which the App is installed.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality changes or if applicable legal or platform requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this webpage and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
